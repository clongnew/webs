Understanding Shaft Kick Point and Torque

By ShaftMaster Staff • April 1, 2024 • 7 min read

Beyond flex, two other critical shaft characteristics significantly impact performance: kick point and torque. Understanding these technical factors can help you fine-tune your club selection for optimal ball flight and shot shape.

What is Kick Point?

Kick point, also known as bend point, is the location on the shaft where it bends the most during the swing. This point determines launch angle and ball flight characteristics:

  • Low Kick Point: Creates higher launch and more spin - ideal for players who need to get the ball in the air
  • Mid Kick Point: Balanced performance for most players
  • High Kick Point: Produces lower launch and less spin - preferred by faster swingers

Understanding Torque

Torque measures how much a shaft twists during the swing. It's measured in degrees and affects stability and feel:

  • Low Torque (2.0-3.0 degrees): More stable, less twisting - preferred by faster swingers
  • Mid Torque (3.0-4.0 degrees): Average stability for most golfers
  • High Torque (4.0+ degrees): More forgiveness, smoother feel - good for slower swings

How Kick Point Affects Shot Shape

Kick point directly influences your ability to shape shots:

  • Higher kick point shafts help reduce slice spin
  • Lower kick point shafts can add draw potential
  • Match kick point to your natural shot shape for best results

Getting the Right Combination

The best way to find the ideal kick point and torque for your game is through professional fitting. A qualified fitter can analyze your launch conditions and recommend the optimal shaft characteristics for your specific needs.
