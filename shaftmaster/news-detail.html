Matrix Introduces Three New Premium Shafts for 2024

By Staff Writer • April 15, 2024 • 5 min read

Matrix Golf has officially unveiled their highly anticipated 2024 shaft lineup, introducing three new premium shafts that feature groundbreaking carbon technology. The new lineup includes the Matrix Platinum, Matrix Black, and Matrix Gold, each designed to meet the specific needs of different golfer profiles.

The Technology Behind the Innovation

What sets the new Matrix shafts apart is their revolutionary Carbon Weave Technology (CWT). This new construction method utilizes a proprietary carbon fiber weaving process that creates a more stable shaft section while maintaining flexibility where it matters most.

"We've spent five years developing this technology," said Matrix's VP of Engineering, Dr. Sarah Chen. "The result is a shaft that provides tour-level stability without sacrificing the feel that better players demand."

Meet the New Lineup

Matrix Platinum

The flagship model in the new lineup, the Platinum is designed for elite players with swing speeds above 105 mph. Key features include:

  • Ultra-high modulus carbon fiber construction
  • Weight: 65g (Stiff), 67g (X-Stiff)
  • Low launch, low spin profile
  • Available flexes: S, X, XX

Matrix Black

The Black is designed for the majority of better players, offering a balance of distance and control:

  • High modulus carbon fiber with composite layering
  • Weight: 60g (Regular), 62g (Stiff)
  • Mid launch, mid spin profile
  • Available flexes: R, S, X

Matrix Gold

Designed for players seeking premium performance at a more accessible price point:

  • Premium graphite with carbon mesh technology
  • Weight: 55g (Regular), 58g (Stiff)
  • Mid-High launch, mid spin profile
  • Available flexes: A, R, S

Preliminary Testing Results

We've had early access to test samples of the new Matrix shafts. Here's what our initial testing revealed:

Matrix Platinum Testing

The Platinum impressed in our distance testing. Testers with swing speeds in the 105+ mph range saw an average increase of 8 yards in carry distance compared to their current shafts, with the added benefit of reduced spin for more roll-out.

Matrix Black Testing

The Black proved to be the most versatile of the new lineup. Golfers across all flex options experienced improved consistency and feel. The mid-launch profile proved popular among testers who wanted a balance of distance and control.

Matrix Gold Testing

The Gold exceeded expectations for its price point. Performance was surprisingly close to shafts costing twice as much, making it an excellent value proposition for golfers looking to upgrade without breaking the bank.

Availability and Pricing

The new Matrix 2024 shafts will be available at authorized retailers starting May 1, 2024. Suggested retail prices are:

  • Matrix Platinum: $450 (driver), $350 (fairway)
  • Matrix Black: $350 (driver), $275 (fairway)
  • Matrix Gold: $225 (driver), $175 (fairway)

Custom club builders will have access to the full lineup immediately, with standard shaft lengths available.

Our Initial Impressions

While we'll reserve full judgment until we complete our comprehensive review, our early impressions are positive. The Matrix 2024 lineup represents a significant step forward in shaft technology.

The Platinum in particular stands out as a potential game-changer for elite players seeking every possible advantage. However, the real story may be the Gold, which brings premium technology to a more accessible price point.

We'll have complete reviews of all three shafts in the coming weeks. Stay tuned to ShaftMaster Pro for our in-depth analysis.

Early Verdict

Matrix has delivered an impressive 2024 lineup that addresses every golfer profile:

  • Elite Players: Look for the Platinum to compete with the best
  • Better Amateurs: The Black offers excellent value
  • Value Seekers: The Gold is a game-changing price point
