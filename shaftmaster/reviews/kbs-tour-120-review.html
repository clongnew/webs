KBS Tour 120 Review

The smoothest heavy-weight steel shaft for iron play precision.

Home / Reviews / KBS Tour 120
Steel Shaft

KBS Tour 120

By Mike Johnson • April 20, 2024 • 10 min read

The KBS Tour 120 is Kim Braly's signature steel shaft and is widely considered one of the best iron shafts available. Known for its smooth feel and higher trajectory, it's a favorite among better players seeking shot versatility.

First Impressions

Right out of the box, the KBS Tour 120 impresses with its black Pearl finish and precision manufacturing. The shaft has a solid, substantial feel with excellent balance between weight and playability.

The shaft features a mid-launch, mid-spin profile that promotes a higher ball flight than its closest competitors. This makes it particularly popular among players who want to attack pins with their long irons.

Build Quality

The build quality of the KBS Tour 120 is exceptional. Each shaft is manufactured with tight tolerances, ensuring consistent performance from club to club. The black Pearl finish is durable and looks professional.

The step pattern is designed to promote smooth loading and consistent feel throughout the set. This is one of the few steel shafts that truly feels the same from 4-iron to PW.

Performance Testing

Distance

Distance with the Tour 120 was consistent, though slightly less than the longest iron shafts on the market. What you lose in distance, you gain in precision and control.

Accuracy

Accuracy is where this shaft excels. The smooth loading promotes consistent face closure, resulting in tight dispersion patterns. Better players will appreciate the ability to shape shots.

Feel

The feel is legendary - smooth, responsive, and incredibly consistent. There's excellent feedback on every shot, allowing players to make instant adjustments based on how the ball feels coming off the face.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Legendary smooth feel
  • Promotes higher trajectory
  • Consistent through the set
  • Excellent feedback
  • Tight dispersion

Cons

  • Not the longest option
  • Premium price
  • May be too light for some

Who Should Play This Shaft?

The KBS Tour 120 is best suited for:

  • Better players seeking shot versatility
  • Golfers who prefer smooth feel
  • Those wanting consistent trajectory
  • Players with moderate swing speeds

Final Verdict

The KBS Tour 120 remains the benchmark for steel iron shafts. Its legendary smooth feel and consistent performance make it a top choice for better players worldwide.

Rating

The KBS Tour 120 earns 5 out of 5 stars for its legendary feel and consistent performance.
