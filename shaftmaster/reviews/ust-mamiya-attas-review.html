UST Mamiya ATTAS

By Mike Johnson • March 18, 2025 • 12 min read

The UST Mamiya ATTAS has been one of the most popular graphite shafts in golf for over a decade. Originally released in 2014, it set a new standard for performance and value that competitors still chase today.

In this comprehensive review, we'll examine every aspect of the ATTAS to help you determine if it's the right shaft for your game.

First Impressions

Right out of the box, the ATTAS impresses with its sleek design and solid construction. Available in weights ranging from 50 to 80 grams, there's an ATTAS option for virtually every swing speed and preference.

The shaft features a mid-launch, mid-spin profile that offers an excellent balance of distance and control. The stiffer butt section helps the shaft keep up with a quicker transition, while the softer tip assists in adding dynamic loft at impact.

Build Quality

The build quality of the ATTAS is exceptional for its price point. UST Mamiya utilizes Japanese Toray carbon fiber and their proprietary Nanoalloy technology to create a shaft that offers Tour-level performance at a more accessible price.

The parallel tip section allows for easy trimming and adjustment, making it a favorite among custom club fitters. The consistent quality from shaft to shaft is remarkable.

Performance Testing

Distance

Distance performance was solid across all weight options. The mid-spin profile helped maintain carry distance while the mid-launch trajectory provided good ball flight visibility. Our testers saw consistent distances across the weight range.

Accuracy

Accuracy was a strong suit. The balanced bend profile promoted consistent face closure, resulting in tight dispersion patterns. Off-center hits were more forgiving than expected for a shaft in this category.

Feel

The feel is best described as smooth and responsive. There's no harshness or vibration, even on mis-hits. The shaft loads progressively, providing excellent feedback through the zone.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Excellent value for performance
  • Multiple weight options
  • Smooth, responsive feel
  • Forgiving on mis-hits
  • Wide availability

Cons

  • Not as stable as premium options
  • Limited workability

Who Should Play This Shaft?

The UST Mamiya ATTAS is best suited for:

  • Golfers seeking quality on a budget
  • Players with swing speeds 85-110 mph
  • Those wanting consistent performance
  • Mid-handicappers improving their game

Final Verdict

The UST Mamiya ATTAS remains one of the best values in graphite shafts. While it may not have the flash of newer premium options, the proven performance and accessible price make it an excellent choice for golfers at every level.
