Mitsubishi Kuro Kage Black Review

The legendary Black Edition returns with improved technology and performance.

Mitsubishi Kuro Kage Black

By Mike Johnson • October 1, 2024 • 12 min read

The Mitsubishi Kuro Kage Black is the latest evolution of one of the most popular shaft families in golf. Building on the success of the original, the Black edition features improved technology and modern design.

First Impressions

Right out of the box, the Kuro Kage Black impresses with its sleek matte black finish and solid construction. The shaft has a substantial feel with excellent balance.

The shaft features a smooth bend profile with a softer mid-section, providing excellent feel while maintaining stability through impact.

Build Quality

The build quality is exceptional. Mitsubishi's reputation for quality is evident in every aspect of the shaft's construction. The carbon fiber layering is uniform and the finish is flawless.

The Dual-Core TiNi technology provides a unique feel that's both stable and responsive.

Performance Testing

Distance

Distance was solid, with a balanced trajectory that worked well for most swing speeds.

Accuracy

Accuracy was excellent. The smooth bend profile promoted consistent face closure.

Feel

The feel is smooth and responsive, with excellent feedback through the zone.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Smooth feel
  • Excellent accuracy
  • Multiple weight options
  • Premium quality

Cons

  • Not the longest option
  • Premium price

Who Should Play This Shaft?

The Mitsubishi Kuro Kage Black is best suited for:

  • Golfers seeking smooth feel
  • Players with swing speeds 85-105 mph
  • Those wanting accuracy
  • Better players

Final Verdict

The Mitsubishi Kuro Kage Black is an excellent all-around shaft that delivers consistent performance. It's a top choice for discerning golfers.

Rating

★★★★☆

The Kuro Kage Black earns 4 out of 5 stars for excellent consistency and feel.
