Mitsubishi Diamana RF

By Mike Johnson • August 27, 2024 • 11 min read

The Mitsubishi Diamana RF (Red Force) is one of the most successful shafts in professional golf. Its high-launch, mid-spin profile has won tournaments worldwide.

First Impressions

Right out of the box, the Diamana RF impresses with its iconic red finish and premium construction. The shaft feels lightweight yet substantial.

The shaft features a high-launch, mid-spin profile that's ideal for maximizing distance while maintaining control.

Technical Specifications

Specification Value
Shaft Weight50-70g
Flex OptionsR, SR, S, X
Torque4.0-5.0°
Tip Diameter0.335"
Launch AngleHigh
Spin LevelMid
Kick PointLow

Build Quality

The build quality is exceptional. Mitsubishi's decades of experience are evident in every aspect of the shaft's construction.

The Multi-Dimensional Interphase (MDI) technology provides unique feel and performance.

Performance Testing

Distance

Distance was excellent, with the high-launch profile helping maximize carry distance.

Accuracy

Accuracy was very good, with tight dispersion patterns.

Feel

The feel is smooth and powerful, with excellent energy transfer.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Tour-proven performance
  • High launch
  • Excellent distance
  • Smooth feel

Cons

  • May be too high-launching for some
  • Premium price

Who Should Play This Shaft?

The Mitsubishi Diamana RF is best suited for:

  • Golfers needing more launch
  • Players with swing speeds 80-100 mph
  • Those wanting Tour performance
  • Moderate swing speeds

Final Verdict

The Mitsubishi Diamana RF is an excellent shaft that's proven at the highest levels. Its high-launch profile makes it ideal for many golfers.

Rating

★★★★☆

The Diamana RF earns 4 out of 5 stars for Tour-proven performance.
