Oban Kiyoshi Tour Limited

By Mike Johnson • April 20, 2024 • 11 min read

The Oban Kiyoshi Tour Limited is a premium shaft crafted in Japan, known for its exceptional feel and consistency. It's a favorite among Tour players and discerning amateurs who demand the best.

First Impressions

Right out of the box, the Kiyoshi Tour Limited impresses with its elegant design and perfect finish. The shaft has a distinctive purple tinted finish that sets it apart from competitors.

The shaft features a high-launch, low-spin profile that provides excellent distance while maintaining control. The stiff butt section ensures stability, while the responsive tip promotes easy loading.

Technical Specifications

Specification Value
Shaft Weight65-75g
Flex OptionsS, X
Torque3.0-3.5°
Tip Diameter0.335"
Launch AngleHigh
Spin LevelLow
Kick PointMid

Build Quality

The build quality is exceptional, as expected from a Japanese-made shaft. Each shaft is individually tested to ensure consistent performance. The carbon fiber layering is precise and uniform.

The Tour Limited features Oban's proprietary bias-axis technology, which positions more mass toward the grip for enhanced stability and control.

Performance Testing

Distance

Distance was impressive, particularly for a shaft that promotes higher launch. The low-spin profile helped maintain roll-out, resulting in excellent total distance.

Accuracy

Accuracy was excellent. The stiff butt section kept the clubhead stable through impact, promoting tight dispersion patterns.

Feel

The feel is extraordinary - smooth and responsive with excellent feedback. Better players will appreciate the ability to shape shots with precision.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Exceptional Japanese craftsmanship
  • Premium feel
  • High launch with low spin
  • Tight dispersion
  • Excellent feedback

Cons

  • Premium price
  • Limited flex options
  • May be too launch-heavy for some

Who Should Play This Shaft?

The Oban Kiyoshi Tour Limited is best suited for:

  • Better players seeking premium quality
  • Golfers who want high launch
  • Those wanting exceptional feel
  • Players with swing speeds 95-110 mph

Final Verdict

The Oban Kiyoshi Tour Limited is an exceptional shaft that delivers premium performance. While the price is high, the Japanese craftsmanship and performance justify the investment.

Rating

★★★★★

The Oban Kiyoshi Tour Limited earns 5 out of 5 stars for exceptional quality and performance.
