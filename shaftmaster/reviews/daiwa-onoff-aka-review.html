DAIWA ONOFF AKA

By Mike Johnson • April 20, 2024 • 10 min read

The DAIWA ONOFF AKA is a premium Japanese shaft that's relatively unknown in Western markets. However, it's been a bestseller in Japan for years, prized for its exceptional quality and performance.

In this review, we'll examine what makes this niche Japanese brand worth considering for serious golfers.

First Impressions

Right out of the box, the ONOFF AKA impresses with its distinctive red finish and premium Japanese craftsmanship. The shaft has a solid, substantial feel with excellent balance.

The shaft features a mid-launch, mid-spin profile that offers excellent versatility. The SMOOTH KICK technology provides unique feel that's both stable and responsive.

Build Quality

The build quality is exceptional, as expected from Japanese manufacturing. DAIWA's decades of experience in fishing equipment translate to golf shafts with unparalleled attention to detail.

The HVF (High Volume Fiber) carbon fiber provides excellent stability while maintaining lightweight performance.

Performance Testing

Distance

Distance was solid, with a balanced trajectory that worked well for most swing speeds. Not the longest, but extremely consistent.

Accuracy

Accuracy was excellent. The stable mid-section helped keep shots online, with tight dispersion patterns.

Feel

The feel is exceptional - smooth and responsive with excellent feedback. The SMOOTH KICK technology provides unique feel that's hard to describe but easy to appreciate.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Japanese precision
  • Exceptional feel
  • Unique SMOOTH KICK technology
  • Tight dispersion
  • Exclusive appeal

Cons

  • Limited Western availability
  • Fewer flex options
  • Hard to find

Who Should Play This Shaft?

The DAIWA ONOFF AKA is best suited for:

  • Golfers seeking exclusive quality
  • Players with swing speeds 85-105 mph
  • Those wanting exceptional feel
  • Collectors of niche equipment

Final Verdict

The DAIWA ONOFF AKA is a hidden gem that's worth seeking out. Its Japanese craftsmanship and unique feel make it a standout choice for discerning golfers willing to look beyond mainstream options.

Rating

★★★★☆

The DAIWA ONOFF AKA earns 4 out of 5 stars for exceptional quality and feel.
