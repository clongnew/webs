Mitsubishi Tensei AV Raw White

By Mike Johnson • April 20, 2024 • 10 min read

The Mitsubishi Chemical Tensei AV Raw White stands as one of the most impressive shafts we've tested in recent years. Used by Rory McIlroy on the PGA Tour, this shaft delivers an extraordinary combination of smoothness and control that sets a new standard for premium graphite shafts.

As part of Mitsubishi's acclaimed Tensei AV Raw series, the White model represents the flagship offering, designed for aggressive swingers who demand Tour-level performance without compromise.

First Impressions

From the first swing, the Tensei AV Raw White makes its presence known. The raw metal finish is striking - a distinctive appearance that sets it apart from conventional shafts. But beyond aesthetics, there's something special happening in your hands.

The feel is remarkably smooth - approaching what we've only previously experienced in the premium Diamana line. There's no harshness, no boardiness, just a silky-smooth interaction between club and ball that inspires confidence on every tee shot.

Testing Results

Our comprehensive testing revealed exceptional performance across multiple metrics:

Consistency

This is where the Tensei AV Raw White truly excels. Shot after shot launched in the same window, with the same spin characteristics and identical shot shape. The consistency is nothing short of remarkable - virtually eliminating the variability that plagues lesser shafts.

Spin Performance

The low spin characteristics place this shaft firmly in the "distance" category. Spin rates consistently stayed in the optimal range, providing excellent distance retention without sacrificing control.

Control & Workability

The stout tip section prevents unpredictable snap hooks, providing exceptional confidence on the tee. Golfers can trust their driver to perform consistently, even on aggressive swings.

Smash Factor

Regularly maxed out smash factor due to consistent strike locations. The shaft promotes center-face contact, translating to maximum distance output.

Real-World Performance

Beyond launch monitor data, we took the Tensei AV Raw White to the course:

Off the Tee

Exceptional performance off the tee. The combination of low spin and smooth feel produced long, piercing drives that seemed to cut through the air. Even on slight mis-hits, the shaft maintained impressive distance.

From the Fairway

The control characteristics translate well to fairway wood play. The shaft provides excellent feedback on quality-of-contact, rewarding clean strikes.

Final Verdict

The Mitsubishi Tensei AV Raw White is a genuine benchmark in modern golf shaft engineering. It delivers the kind of performance that makes you wonder how you ever settled for less.

Who should play this shaft:

  • Aggressive swingers with 100+ mph clubhead speed
  • Golfers seeking Tour-level performance
  • Players who prioritize consistency over forgiveness
  • Those wanting to maximize smash factor

If you're serious about optimizing your driving performance and your swing speed supports it, the Tensei AV Raw White deserves serious consideration. It's our top recommendation for premium graphite shafts in 2024.
