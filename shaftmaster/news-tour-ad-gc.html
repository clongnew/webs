Graphite Design Tour AD GC - Game Changer Released

By ShaftMaster Staff • September 6, 2024 • 4 min read

Graphite Design has released the new 2025 Graphite Design Tour AD GC (Game Changer) wood shafts, available beginning September 6, 2024, through Pro's Choice Golf Shafts, Inc. This latest addition to the Tour AD family promises to change how golfers approach their long game.

Game Changer Technology

The Tour AD GC represents a new approach to shaft design, optimized for players seeking maximum distance without sacrificing feel. The "Game Changer" name reflects Graphite Design's confidence that this shaft will significantly improve performance for a wide range of golfers.

Performance Characteristics

The Tour AD GC features a mid-launch, mid-spin profile that delivers excellent distance while maintaining control. Available in multiple weight options, it accommodates various swing speeds and player preferences.

Key features include:

  • Mid-launch, mid-spin profile
  • Tour AD series construction
  • Multiple weight options
  • Available in 5F, 6F, 7F, 8F flex

Availability

The Tour AD GC is now available through authorized Graphite Design dealers and fitters.
