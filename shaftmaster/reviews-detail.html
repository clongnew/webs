Project X HZRDUS Black 60 Review

Premium graphite shaft for moderate speed swingers seeking distance and stability.

Project X HZRDUS Black 60

By Mike Johnson • April 18, 2024 • 12 min read

The Project X HZRDUS Black 60 has been a staple in the graphite shaft market for several years, and for good reason. This premium shaft offers a unique combination of stability, distance, and consistency that appeals to a wide range of golfers.

In this comprehensive review, we'll break down every aspect of the HZRDUS Black 60 to help you determine if it's the right shaft for your game.

First Impressions

Right out of the box, the HZRDUS Black 60 impresses with its sleek matte black finish and premium feel. The shaft has a solid, substantial feel without being overly heavy, and the balance point is well-positioned for most clubhead speeds in the 90-105 mph range.

The shaft features a low-launch, low-spin profile that has become increasingly popular among better players seeking to maximize distance while maintaining control.

Testing Methodology

Our testing process is designed to provide unbiased, repeatable data. We test each shaft in the following conditions:

  • Test Club: Driver (9° loft)
  • Test Environment: Indoor launch monitor (TrackMan)
  • Test Samples: 3 shafts of each flex
  • Tester Profile: Multiple testers with swing speeds ranging from 85-110 mph
  • Total Swings: 100+ per shaft

Our Testing Results

We put the HZRDUS Black 60 through extensive testing with our team of testers. Here's what we found:

Distance Performance

The HZRDUS Black 60 excelled in distance, particularly for a mid-weight graphite shaft. Our testers with swing speeds in the 95-105 mph range saw average carry distances of 230-245 yards with good roll-out.

Accuracy & Consistency

This is where the HZRDUS truly shines. The shaft provides exceptional consistency from shot to shot, with tight dispersion patterns. Our testing showed very minimal deviation in both direction and distance.

Feel

The feel of the HZRDUS Black 60 is best described as "solid but not harsh." It provides excellent feedback without being too stiff or boardy. The shaft has a smooth loading feel with a responsive tip section.

Real-World Performance

Beyond launch monitor numbers, we took the HZRDUS Black 60 to the course for real-world testing. Here's how it performed:

Off the Tee

The low-spin characteristics make this shaft an excellent choice for drivers. Golfers who struggle with excess spin will appreciate the ability to work the ball both ways while maintaining distance.

On Approach Shots

For mid-iron approaches, the shaft provides good feel and feedback. The relatively stiff tip allows for precise distance control, though some golfers may prefer a slightly softer shaft for touch shots around the green.

In Rough Conditions

The HZRDUS Black 60 performs admirably from rough lies. The shaft's stability helps maintain clubface control even on mis-hit shots.

Final Verdict

The Project X HZRDUS Black 60 is an excellent choice for golfers with moderate to fast swing speeds (90+ mph) who prioritize distance and accuracy. It's particularly well-suited for:

  • Competitive golfers seeking tour-level performance
  • Golfers who struggle with excess spin
  • Low handicappers looking to maximize distance
  • Players who value consistency over forgiveness

If you're in the market for a premium graphite shaft and your swing speed supports it, the HZRDUS Black 60 should be at the top of your consideration list. It's earned our Editor's Choice award for 2024.
