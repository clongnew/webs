Graphite Design Tour AD FI Announced for 2025

By ShaftMaster Staff • April 5, 2025 • 4 min read

Graphite Design has announced that the new Tour AD FI (Flight Intelligence) shafts will be available beginning September 5, 2025, through Pro's Choice Golf Shafts, Inc. This latest addition to the prestigious Tour AD family promises to deliver unparalleled performance through innovative engineering.

Flight Intelligence Technology

The Tour AD FI introduces Graphite Design's new Flight Intelligence technology, which optimizes launch conditions and spin rates for maximum distance and accuracy. This technology has been developed through extensive player feedback and testing at the highest levels of professional golf.

According to the company, the Tour AD FI has a similar character to the popular Tour AD PT and Tour AD TP but with an added tightness that better players have been requesting. This makes it ideal for golfers who want workability without sacrificing distance.

Availability

The Tour AD FI will be released in multiple weight configurations to accommodate various swing speeds. Watch for more details as the release date approaches.
