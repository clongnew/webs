KBS Dominates June 2024 LPGA Tour with PGI Shafts

By GShaftMaster Staff • July 5, 2024 • 4 min read

KBS Golf Shafts has announced a remarkable achievement as their PGI (Players Graphite Iron) shafts dominated the LPGA Tour throughout June 2024. The success marks a significant milestone for the brand as professional golfers continue to embrace the steel-like performance of graphite iron shafts.

Unmatched Performance on Tour

Throughout June 2024, KBS shafts featured prominently in winner's circles across LPGA Tour events. The PGI shaft, designed to provide unmatched shot making and control in a graphite iron shaft, proved its worth at the highest level of professional golf.

The PGI Difference

The KBS PGI was designed by Director of R&D and master shaft maker Kim Braly, with the goal of creating a graphite iron shaft that provides steel-like performance. The key features include:

  • Steel-like stability and control
  • Consistent feel throughout the set
  • Optimized launch characteristics
  • Versatile performance for all skill levels

Industry Recognition

The June 2024 LPGA Tour dominance follows extensive growth in the PGI line throughout 2024. KBS has seen increasing adoption from both professional players and amateur golfers seeking to improve their iron performance with graphite technology.
