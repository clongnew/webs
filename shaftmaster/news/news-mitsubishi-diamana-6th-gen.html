Mitsubishi Golf Launches 6th Generation Diamana Series for 2025

By GShaftMaster Staff • March 10, 2025 • 6 min read

Mitsubishi Golf has officially unveiled the 6th Generation Diamana shaft series, completing the latest evolution of their flagship graphite shaft line. The new Diamana series represents the most significant advancement in the legendary Diamana family since its inception in 2004.

A Legacy of Excellence

Since its introduction nearly two decades ago, Diamana has become the foundation of Mitsubishi Golf and has become the most winningest graphite driver shaft in professional golf history. The 6th Generation continues this tradition while introducing breakthrough technologies that address the evolving needs of modern golfers.

Enhanced Carbon Fiber Technology

The 6th Generation Diamana features enhanced carbon fiber layering for better shaft stability. This improvement allows golfers with varying swing tempos and speeds to experience optimal performance. The new construction utilizes high-strength DIALEAD Pitch Fiber in an updated configuration, providing unprecedented strength and control during shaft loading and unloading.

Key technological advancements include:

  • Improved carbon fiber layup for enhanced stability
  • Optimized bend profile for smoother feel
  • Expanded weight options to fit more golfers
  • Advanced materials reducing unwanted shaft deformation

Available Models

The 6th Generation Diamana series will be available in multiple profiles to match various player preferences and swing characteristics. The lineup includes options for players seeking higher launch, lower spin, and everything in between.

Availability

The 6th Generation Diamana shafts will be available at authorized Mitsubishi Golf dealers and custom fitting locations beginning spring 2025. Players are encouraged to get fitted by a certified fitter to find the ideal shaft for their game.
