Aldila Introduces ROGUE Black Hybrid Shaft

By GShaftMaster Staff • December 15, 2024 • 4 min read

Aldila is set to introduce a brand-new hybrid shaft to their already formidable ROGUE series: The ROGUE Black Hybrid. This new offering is built on the same core technologies that made the ROGUE Black 130 M.S.I. successful, specifically designed to complement the recently released driver and fairway wood shaft.

Designed for Hybrid Performance

The ROGUE Black Hybrid is described as a workable, Mid/High launching, Mid Spinning shaft designed to complement the ROGUE Black 130 M.S.I. It addresses the specific demands of hybrid clubs, which require different performance characteristics compared to woods.

Core Technologies

The new hybrid shaft incorporates the same advanced technologies that define the ROGUE Black family:

  • M.S.I. (Massive Stability Increase) technology for enhanced stability
  • Optimized launch characteristics for mid/high trajectory
  • Mid spin performance for controlled distance
  • Consistent feel across the set

Availability

The Aldila ROGUE Black Hybrid will be available beginning December 2024 through authorized Aldila dealers and custom fitting locations worldwide.
