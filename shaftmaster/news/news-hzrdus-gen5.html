Project X HZRDUS Gen 5 Black Released

By ShaftMaster Staff • November 15, 2024 • 4 min read

True Temper has released the next generation of the legendary HZRDUS shaft with the new Project X HZRDUS Gen 5 Black. This latest iteration builds upon the success of previous generations while introducing new technology for enhanced performance.

Generation 5 Improvements

The Gen 5 Black features an updated carbon fiber layout and improved resin system that provides better energy transfer and consistency. The low-spin, low-launch profile that made the original HZRDUS Black famous has been refined for even better performance.

According to early testers, the Gen 5 Black maintains the signature HZRDUS feel while offering improved stability and a slightly smoother impact sensation. The shaft is designed for today's aggressive swings and provides the low spin that advanced players prefer.

Key Features

  • Low-launch, low-spin profile
  • 60 gram model in 6.5 flex
  • Updated carbon fiber technology
  • Enhanced stability

Availability

The HZRDUS Gen 5 Black is now available at authorized dealers and fitting locations worldwide.
