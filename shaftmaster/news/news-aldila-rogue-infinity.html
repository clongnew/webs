Aldila Launches Revolutionary ROGUE Infinity Shaft for 2025

By GShaftMaster Staff • January 20, 2025 • 5 min read

Aldila has officially launched the ROGUE Infinity (ROGUE ∞), the latest addition to their acclaimed ROGUE shaft family. This new shaft introduces the revolutionary Dual Torque Core technology, which the company describes as delivering the best feel in ROGUE history while maintaining the extreme counterbalanced performance that the series is known for.

Dual Torque Core Technology

The ROGUE Infinity represents a major technological advancement for Aldila. The innovative Dual Torque Core uses 80 Ton Pitch Fiber in a full-length application combined with 46 Ton Pan Fiber in the angle plys to drive down torque and create exceptional stability throughout the shaft.

"The new ROGUE Infinity is an extremely counterbalanced shaft but also its best feeling, attributable to Aldila's all-new innovative Dual Torque Core," said an Aldila spokesperson. "This technology represents years of development and testing to achieve the perfect balance of performance and feel."

Performance Characteristics

The ROGUE Infinity is designed for players who demand maximum distance and stability from their equipment. The key performance features include:

  • Enhanced stability through improved torque control
  • Smooth feel throughout the swing
  • Consistent energy transfer for greater distance
  • Available in multiple weight options

Availability

The Aldila ROGUE Infinity is now available at authorized Aldila dealers worldwide. The shaft comes with adapter tip options for most major driver heads and is also available in PURE®d configuration for optimal assembly.
